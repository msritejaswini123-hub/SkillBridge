What happens to your file

  1. We verify it is genuinely a PDF, not just named .pdf.
  2. pypdf extracts the text of every page.
  3. The text is matched against {{ library_size }} skills including spelling variants — sklearn becomes Scikit-learn.
  4. We also look for your degree, branch, months of experience and projects.
  5. You review and edit everything on the next screen.

The file stays on this machine in the project's uploads/ folder. Only you can open it.
