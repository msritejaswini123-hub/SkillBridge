What happens to your file

  1. We check it is genuinely a PDF (not just named .pdf).
  2. pypdf extracts the text of every page.
  3. The text is matched against a dictionary of {{ 89 }}+ skills, including spelling variants — sklearn becomes Scikit-learn, HTML5 becomes HTML.
  4. We also look for your degree, branch, months of experience and your projects section.
  5. You review and edit everything on the next screen before it is saved.

Your file stays on this machine, in the project's uploads/ folder. Only you can open it.
